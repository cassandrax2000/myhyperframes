# SnoutSwipe Walkthrough — Design

## Style Prompt
Warm, friendly app demo. Sage green and off-white palette from the SnoutSwipe brand. Clean, modern sans-serif. Soft glow backgrounds and gentle motion — inviting, not flashy.

## Colors
- `#EAE7DF` — warm off-white, canvas background
- `#C5CBAA` — sage green, brand accent
- `#1A1A1A` — near-black, text and phone frame
- `#FFFFFF` — white, card and screen backgrounds
- `#7A806A` — muted sage, secondary text and captions

## Typography
- `Figtree` — single sans-serif family; 800 for headlines, 400–500 for body. Warm, geometric, not on the banned list.

## What NOT to Do
- No gradient text
- No cyan/purple/neon accents
- No centered-everything layouts with equal visual weight
- No drop shadows on text
- No exit animations before screen transitions (push-slide handles the exit)
